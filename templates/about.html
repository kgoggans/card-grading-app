About PSA Grading

What is PSA Grading?

PSA (Professional Sports Authenticator) is the world's largest third-party authentication and grading company for trading cards and collectibles. PSA uses a 1-10 grading scale to assess the condition of sports cards.

The PSA Grading Scale

Grade Designation Description
10 Gem Mint A PSA Gem Mint 10 card is a virtually perfect card with sharp corners, good centering, and no staining.
9 Mint A PSA Mint 9 is a superb condition card with only one minor flaw upon close inspection.
8 Near Mint-Mint A PSA NM-MT 8 shows very slight wear on corners, edges, or surface but still has a pristine appearance.
7 Near Mint A PSA NM 7 has minor imperfections but is still quite attractive.
6 Excellent-Mint A PSA EX-MT 6 shows minor wear on corners and edges but is still very collectible.
5 Excellent A PSA EX 5 has moderate wear but is still relatively attractive.
4 Very Good-Excellent A PSA VG-EX 4 shows obvious wear but no major creases.
3 Very Good A PSA VG 3 has noticeable wear with possible light creases.
2 Good A PSA Good 2 shows substantial wear with creases and other damage.
1 Poor A PSA Poor 1 has heavy wear and damage.

How Our AI Grading Works

Our AI-powered grading system analyzes uploaded images of sports cards and estimates the PSA grade based on multiple factors:

Key Grading Criteria

  • Corners (25%): Examines corner sharpness, wear, and rounding
  • Edges (20%): Checks for chipping, whitening, and roughness
  • Surface (25%): Looks for scratches, creases, stains, and print defects
  • Centering (15%): Analyzes image placement and border balance
  • Print Quality (15%): Evaluates focus, color accuracy, and registration

Analysis Process

  1. Images are uploaded (front and back of the card)
  2. Computer vision algorithms analyze each grading criterion
  3. Both sides are evaluated (card receives the lower score if sides differ)
  4. Category scores are weighted and combined for an overall grade
  5. Results include detailed breakdown and explanations

Important Notes on Accuracy

For best results:

  • Use high-resolution images (at least 800px on the smallest side)
  • Ensure good, even lighting without glare or shadows
  • Capture the entire card with clear borders visible
  • Take photos on a contrasting background
  • Avoid using flash which can create glare

Limitations:

  • This is an AI estimate and NOT an official PSA grade
  • Actual PSA grading considers factors not visible in photos
  • Image quality directly impacts accuracy
  • Some card-specific issues may not be detectable
  • For official grading and authentication, submit to PSA directly

Achieving Consistent Results

To get the most accurate and consistent grading estimates:

  • Consistent Photography: Use the same lighting setup and camera angle for all cards
  • High Quality Images: Higher resolution images provide more detail for analysis
  • Clean Cards: Remove any dust or fingerprints before photographing
  • Multiple Angles: While the app requires front/back, ensuring both images are clear helps
  • Flat Surface: Photograph cards on a flat surface to avoid distortion

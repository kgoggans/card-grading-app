About Custom Grading System

Custom 100-1000 Point Grading Scale

This application uses a comprehensive custom grading system with a 100-1000 point scale, providing much more granular assessment than traditional 1-10 scales. The system evaluates 19 distinct grade levels from POOR to PRISTINE condition.

Grading Categories

Each card is evaluated across four key categories, each weighted equally at 25%:

1. Corners (25%)

  • Evaluates corner sharpness, wear, and rounding
  • Checks for corner touches, fraying, and surface compromise
  • Ranges from pristine sharp corners to misshaped/fallen off

2. Edges (25%)

  • Examines edge condition for chipping, whitening, and roughness
  • Looks for notches, tears, and fill artifacts
  • Assesses edge surface wear and integrity

3. Surface (25%)

  • Analyzes scratches, creases, wrinkles, and stains
  • Checks for print defects and imperfections
  • Evaluates overall surface integrity and gloss retention

4. Centering (25%)

  • Measures image placement within borders
  • Compares left/right and top/bottom ratios
  • Ranges from perfect 51/49 to severely off-center 95/5

How Grading Works

  1. Image Upload: Both front and back images are uploaded
  2. Computer Vision Analysis: AI algorithms analyze each category
  3. Category Scoring: Each category receives a score from 100-1000
  4. Dual-Side Evaluation: Both sides are analyzed; card receives the lower score per category
  5. Weighted Combination: Category scores are combined using equal 25% weights
  6. Final Grade: Overall score maps to one of 19 grade levels

Important Notes

  • This is an AI estimate, not an official professional grade
  • Image quality directly impacts grading accuracy
  • Use high-resolution, well-lit images for best results
  • Scores are for informational and comparative purposes
  • Physical card condition may differ from what's visible in photos

Tips for Accurate Grading

  • Lighting: Use even, natural lighting without glare or shadows
  • Resolution: Capture images at 800px minimum on smallest dimension
  • Focus: Ensure card is in sharp focus across entire surface
  • Background: Use contrasting, solid-color background
  • Positioning: Keep camera parallel to card for accurate centering assessment
  • Coverage: Include entire card with all borders visible
